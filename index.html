01Kaukira · La Mosquitia, Honduras

Where the story starts

I grew up in Kaukira (Cauquira on some maps), a small Miskito village on the shore of the Caratasca Lagoon in La Mosquitia, the far eastern corner of Honduras.

Life there moves with the water. Houses stand on stilts, the lagoon is the main road, and a cayuco, a dugout canoe, is how you get around. It's a long way, in every sense, from a computer screen.

02UTH · Honduras

Discovering code

I left the coast to study at Universidad Tecnológica de Honduras (UTH), a private university, and graduated as a computer engineer.

Telling a machine exactly what to do, and watching it do it, felt like magic. It still does.

03UNITEC · San Pedro Sula

A decade at the front of the classroom

After graduating I joined Universidad Tecnológica Centroamericana (UNITEC) as an instructor, and taught there for twelve years. I taught web development, IT for business and Lean Startup methods, and mentored students building their first products and companies.

Along the way I earned a Master's degree in Project Management at UNITEC, and learned that explaining something simply is the best way to really understand it.

04Acklen Avenue · Remote from San Pedro Sula

Working for the world from home

I joined Acklen Avenue as a full-stack developer consultant, working remotely from San Pedro Sula for mostly US-based clients with distributed, agile teams.

Node.jsPostgreSQLReactReact NativeAWS

It showed me that where you start doesn't decide how far your work can travel.

05Honduras → Sweden

The biggest jump

Then I applied for a job in Sweden and moved to Göteborg to work as a consultant.

A new language, new seasons, winter days that end in the middle of the afternoon, and a whole new chapter.
